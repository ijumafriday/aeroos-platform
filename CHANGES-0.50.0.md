# AeroOS 0.50.0 — Quality Risk & Finding Classification

- Adds an explicit server-side 5x5 Quality Risk & Finding Classification Engine using likelihood × compliance/quality impact.
- Adds LOW / MEDIUM / HIGH / CRITICAL classification, audited reassessment, recurrence, classification basis and containment controls to quality findings.
- HIGH and CRITICAL findings require documented containment and receive tighter response windows; closure retains root-cause and verified-CAPA gates.
- Adds an explicit cross-audit Finding & Non-Compliance Tracker with status/risk filters, overdue visibility, CAPA state and audit/requirement traceability.
- Preserves the existing audit, finding, CAPA, surveillance and compliance workflows; no duplicate non-compliance datastore is introduced.
